What is the difference between a Sprint backlog and a Product Backlog?

The Product Backlog is an ordered list of features, improvements, fixes, and other work that may be needed for a product. The Product Owner is responsible for managing the Product Backlog and helping the team understand which items are most important. The backlog can change as the team learns more about the product and its users.

The Sprint Backlog is the set of Product Backlog items selected for the current Sprint, together with the plan for delivering them and achieving the Sprint Goal. Developers use the Sprint Backlog to organize and track their work during the Sprint. Unlike the Product Backlog, which covers potential work for the product as a whole, the Sprint Backlog focuses on the work the team plans to complete during one Sprint.

For example, a team building a Scrum learning website might have many possible topics in its Product Backlog. For one Sprint, the team might select the Sprint Backlog and Sprint execution webpages to work on. As the team makes progress, it can update its Sprint Backlog plan to reflect what it learns and what work remains.
