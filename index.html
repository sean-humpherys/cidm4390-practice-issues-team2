What is product backlog grooming?

Product backlog grooming is the ongoing work of refining, re-prioritizing, and clarifying items in the Product Backlog so the team is always ready for the next Sprint. Learn why it is a team activity and how it keeps Sprints running smoothly.

What is Daily Scrum, who participates, and what is the objective? What is not Daily Scrum?

The Daily Scrum is a 15-minute event for the Developers to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog. Find out who attends, what the goal is, and what common practices (like status reports to a manager) are not part of the Daily Scrum.

What is a Sprint Review activity?

The Sprint Review is an informal meeting at the end of the Sprint where the Scrum Team and stakeholders inspect the Increment and adapt the Product Backlog. Discover what happens during the review and why it is more than just a demo.

What is a Sprint Retrospective activity?

The Sprint Retrospective is the team's chance to inspect how the last Sprint went and identify improvements for the next one. Learn the purpose, format, and outcomes of this continuous improvement event.

What is the difference between a Sprint backlog and a Product Backlog?

The Product Backlog is the master list of everything that might go into the product; the Sprint Backlog is the subset the team commits to for one Sprint, plus the plan for delivering it. This post breaks down the two and how they relate.

What is Sprint execution?

Sprint execution is the work the team does during the Sprint to turn Sprint Backlog items into a potentially shippable product Increment. Learn what happens day to day and how the team self-organizes to get it done.

Kenneth S. Rubin is a well-known Scrum trainer, author, and coach, best known for his book Essential Scrum and the Visual AGILExicon. Learn how his work helps teams understand Scrum quickly.

A DevOps professional bridges development and operations, automating the delivery pipeline so each Sprint's product Increment can be deployed quickly and reliably. Learn how CI/CD and collaboration fit into a Scrum team.
