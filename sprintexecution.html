What is Sprint execution?

Sprint execution is the period during a Scrum Sprint when the team performs the work needed to achieve its Sprint Goal. After Sprint Planning, team members design, build, test, and integrate product features. The team uses the Sprint Backlog to organize and track its work. Sprint execution focuses on completing valuable work while maintaining the quality of the product.

During sprint execution, Scrum team members communicate regularly, solve problems, and adjust their plans as they learn more about the work. The Daily Scrum gives Developers an opportunity to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt their plan for the next day. For example, if a team is building a website to teach Scrum, one developer might create a page explaining the Product Backlog while another develops a page about Sprint execution. The team checks its progress and helps resolve problems so the work can be completed successfully.

Sprint execution is not just about finishing individual tasks; it is about working together to deliver a usable, high-quality product increment. Team members should communicate obstacles early, help one another, and make sure completed work meets the team's Definition of Done. At the end of the Sprint, the team reviews the completed increment and gathers feedback to help guide future work.
