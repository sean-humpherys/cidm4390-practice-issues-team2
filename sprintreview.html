What do you want to know?

The Sprint Review is a Scrum event held near the end of each Sprint where the Scrum Team and stakeholders review the work that has been completed. The main purpose of the Sprint Review is to inspect the outcome of the Sprint and discuss what should happen next. During this meeting, the team demonstrates completed work, gathers feedback, and discusses whether the product is meeting the needs of the customer.

The Sprint Review involves the Scrum Team, which includes the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers, along with important stakeholders. During the meeting, the team discusses what was accomplished during the Sprint and any changes that may affect the product. Stakeholders can provide feedback, ask questions, and suggest improvements. The Product Backlog may then be adjusted based on the feedback and new priorities.

For example, imagine a team developing a music streaming website. During the Sprint Review, the Developers demonstrate a new feature that allows users to create and organize playlists. Stakeholders might test the feature and suggest adding an option to sort songs by artist or genre. The team can use this feedback to update the Product Backlog and decide what improvements should be considered in future Sprints. The Sprint Review is not just a presentation or a meeting to approve completed work, but an opportunity for collaboration and improving the product.
