Who is Kenneth S. Rubin? What does he know about Scrum?

Kenneth S. Rubin is an author, educator, and experienced leader in the Agile and Scrum community. He is widely known for helping organizations understand Scrum and apply it to their work. His writing explains Scrum concepts in practical terms for teams, Scrum Masters, and product owners.

Rubin wrote Essential Scrum: A Practical Guide to the Most Popular Agile Process, a book that explains Scrum roles, events, artifacts, and practices. It is intended to help people understand how Scrum works and how teams can use it to deliver value through iterative development and continuous feedback.

Rubin is also associated with the Visual AGILExicon, a visual resource for explaining Agile and Scrum concepts. These visuals can help teams discuss ideas, build a shared understanding, and communicate Scrum practices more clearly.
