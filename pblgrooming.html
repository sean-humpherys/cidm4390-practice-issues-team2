What is product backlog grooming?

Product Backlog Grooming, also known as Product Backlog Refinement, is an ongoing activity in Scrum where the Product Owner and Developers review and improve items in the Product Backlog. The purpose is to make sure backlog items are clear, organized, and ready for future Sprint Planning. During refinement, the team may add details, break down larger tasks, and estimate how much work each item will take.

During Product Backlog Grooming, the team discusses which backlog items are most important and whether they need additional information. The Product Owner is responsible for ordering the Product Backlog, while Developers help estimate the effort required to complete the work. This helps the Scrum Team understand what needs to be done and reduces confusion when planning the next Sprint.

For example, imagine a team developing a music streaming website. One backlog item might be "Allow users to create playlists." During refinement, the team could break this into smaller tasks, such as creating a playlist, naming it, and adding songs. The team can then discuss the requirements and estimate the work needed for each task. This makes it easier to select and complete backlog items during future Sprints.
